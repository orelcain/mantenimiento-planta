TÍTULO: The Girl at the Threshold

PERSONAJES:
  YUNA: korean girl 20 years old, long straight black hair, light gray eyes, white and navy blue school uniform, pale skin
  SOMBRA: faceless dark figure, long black hooded cloak, blurred silhouette, glowing red eyes, tall and ominous

ESCENA 1
FONDO: empty high school hallway in South Korea, late afternoon, warm orange light streaming through large windows, rows of blue lockers, dusty silence
NARRADOR: Every day, Yuna arrived last to the empty hallway.
YUNA: Is there anyone there?

ESCENA 2
FONDO: same hallway growing darker, fluorescent lights flickering, long shadows stretching across the floor tiles
SOMBRA: I always knew you would come.
YUNA: That's impossible. I've never seen you before.
NARRADOR: But her hands trembled as if she recognized him from a dream.

ESCENA 3
FONDO: school rooftop at night, Seoul city lights glowing below, strong wind blowing through Yuna's hair, full moon above
NARRADOR: The city lights blurred through her tears.
YUNA: Why does everything feel like a memory I never had?
SOMBRA: Because it is. You just forgot.

ESCENA 4
FONDO: same rooftop, Yuna stepping back toward the ledge, the dark figure extending one shadowy hand, stars visible behind the clouds
YUNA: Who are you really?
SOMBRA: The part of you that remembers everything.
NARRADOR: And in that moment, the boundary between past and present dissolved.
